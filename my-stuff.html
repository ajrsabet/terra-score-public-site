A look at how the everyday parts of life add up — and which ones actually move the needle. This page uses an example household so you can see the idea in action.

Example data, not your account

Why estimates instead of exact numbers

Nobody can measure their own footprint precisely, and any tool that claims otherwise is overselling. What is useful is the relative picture: knowing that home energy and travel usually dwarf the smaller daily choices helps you spend your effort where it counts.

As the platform grows, these categories will connect to the same evidence-linked product data used elsewhere on the site, so the numbers behind each item can be traced and checked.
