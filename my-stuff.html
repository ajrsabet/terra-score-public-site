What these comparisons can and cannot tell you

The sample profile is illustrative. The US and global aggregate greenhouse-gas references are sourced 2023 territorial averages from Our World in Data, including land-use change; the example profile uses a different accounting basis, so that chart is broad context rather than a like-for-like ranking.

The category percentile sliders are fictional placeholders invented to demonstrate a future comparison. They do not represent actual people, population distributions, or verified research.

The $27,400 figure is a fictional design example, not a verified estimate, bill, or claim about actual harm caused by a person or community. No calculation supports it. Real estimates would require reviewed evidence, transparent methods, defined boundaries, and uncertainty ranges across climate damages, labor and wage gaps, ecosystem loss, restoration, and other impacts.
