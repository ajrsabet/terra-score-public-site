Project overview

Transparent sustainability data for people who need evidence, not black boxes.

TerraScore is being built as public-interest infrastructure for collecting and reviewing sustainability evidence across products, organizations, materials, processes, and impacts.

The early audience is research institutions, nonprofits, mission-aligned pilot partners, and reporting teams that need structured lifecycle data with provenance. Consumer comparison remains a long-term goal after the data layer is dense enough to support public trust.
