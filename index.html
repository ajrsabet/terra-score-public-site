Why this matters now

Sustainability claims are fragmented across vendors, studies, certifications, and inconsistent reporting systems.

The platform connects measurements, sources, lifecycle stages, and explainable scoring into a reviewable system instead of a black-box rating.

Early institutional adoption helps build the dataset required for later public trust and consumer usefulness.

Who this is for first

The first users should be the ones who strengthen data quality, test the evidence model, and improve the credibility of the mission.

Research Institutions

Use structured lifecycle evidence, linked sources, and explainable scoring to support comparative studies, internal tools, and shared sustainability datasets.

ESG and Reporting Teams

Build auditable evidence trails for sustainability claims and reporting workflows without depending on opaque scoring systems.

Public-Interest Partners

Collaborate on open data infrastructure, governance, and verification systems that improve sustainability transparency globally.

What already exists in the MVP

Development has moved beyond a placeholder info page. The current product includes core contribution, review, and scoring infrastructure that can support early pilots and data collaboration.

The live site should communicate that clearly: this is not yet a consumer comparison destination, but it is already an operating system for evidence-driven sustainability data.

Evidence Collection

Review Workflow

Moderation and revision history preserve provenance so published data remains reviewable and auditable.

Explainable Scores

Model-versioned scores include confidence, completeness, category views, and improvement opportunities.
