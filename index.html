What is Shared Footprint?

I am building an environmental impact assessment tool to identify the environmental and social consequences of the products we make, use, and depend on. This platform provides a framework where the footprint of products and companies are scored.

Why do we need Shared Footprint?

We have more information than ever...

  1. Scientific research: Thousands of studies contain pieces of the puzzle.
  2. Government data: Agencies collect enormous amounts of environmental information.
  3. Corporate reporting: Companies publish increasingly detailed sustainability information.

Seeing the bigger picture is still difficult. Different sources measure different things, use different assumptions, and cover different parts of a product's lifecycle. The information exists; however, we need a better way to connect it.

How will Shared Footprint work?

Shared Footprint maps the entire lifecycle of products and companies, connecting information about materials, production, transportation, use, and disposal – and traces how each stage impacts the environment and society. It will accomplish this by crowdsourcing information from researchers, companies, and individuals, and by providing a framework for evaluating the quality of that information.

Why I'm doing this

I've spent much of my career working with complicated systems—designing buildings, managing construction projects, solving problems, and figuring out how different pieces fit together.

As I've studied environmental science and software development, I've become increasingly interested in a different kind of problem: how do we make sense of everything we already know about our impact on the planet — and actually use it to do better?
