Lifecycle and score explanation

How TerraScore follows a product

TerraScore connects public product scores to evidence across the full lifecycle. The summary is designed for quick comparison; the research workspace contains the detailed records, sources, assumptions, and review history.

1
Raw materials

Extraction and processing of materials, energy, water, and other inputs.

2
Manufacturing

Production, assembly, packaging, and process-specific evidence.

3
Use

Energy, maintenance, service life, and real-world usage assumptions.

4
End of life

Reuse, repair, recycling, recovery, disposal, and remaining uncertainty.

Overall impact score

A 0-100 comparison score summarizes the twelve governed impact categories. Confidence, absolute footprint, true-cost ranges, and source evidence remain separate so the summary does not hide tradeoffs.
